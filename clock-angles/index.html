THE MODEL

A continuous clock

Both hands move smoothly. The minute hand travels at 0.1°/s; the hour hand at 1/120°/s. We record their smaller separation at every whole second, including midnight and noon.

THE EQUATION

θ(t) = min(r, 360 − r)

Here r = (11t/120) mod 360, with t in seconds after midnight and angles in degrees. The smaller angle always lies between 0° and 180°.

WHAT TO NOTICE

A meeting every ~65 min

The pattern repeats every 43,200/11 seconds. There are 11 full cycles. Most exact meetings and 180° peaks fall between recorded seconds; the line connects the samples. Zoom in to see individual points.
